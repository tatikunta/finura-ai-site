The problem

AI agents can generate.
They can't yet be trusted to act.

They can't reason

They can't remember

No durable memory or context across tasks.

They can't prove decisions

Alpha Triad

The Alpha Triad

Three agents in a closed loop: plan, act, check — and re-plan when something is off.

Planner

Decomposes goals into executable sub-tasks.

Executor

Carries out approved steps with tools, APIs and memory.

Critic

Checks each step against policy, pauses for human approval, records a tamper-evident audit, and triggers re-planning.
